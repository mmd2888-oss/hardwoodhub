The WNBA is entering its 30th year with 15 teams, two new cities, and a generation of fans who built this thing from the ground up. Season ticket holders who couldn't make a game used to leave their seats empty — or scramble through bots and resellers.

Not anymore. Hardwood Hub is the exchange we always needed: fan-to-fan, team-color-coded, with a rep system that rewards the community builders. No corporate middlemen. No bots. Just us.

How It Works

STRAIGHTFORWARD.
NO DRAMA.

Verify once, then list, offer, and close deals. Payment comes through within 24 hours of completion. That's it.

1
✅

Verify

Upload your season ticket card or proof of purchase. One-time review, then you're in. Verified badge on your profile.

2
🎟️

List

Post your seats, parking, club access, or merch. No fee to list. Set your price or mark it as a trade.

3
🤝

Deal

Make an offer, go back and forth, confirm the terms. Both fans see a deal summary page when you're locked in.

4
💸

Get Paid

Transfer your ticket through your team's app, mark it sent. Buyer confirms received. Done — payment lands within 24 hours.

How It Works

STRAIGHTFORWARD.
NO DRAMA.

Verify once, then list, offer, and close deals. Payment comes through within 24 hours of completion.

1

Verify

Upload your season ticket card and add a card on file. One-time manual review — once approved, you're a verified member and can list or offer.

2

List

Post your seats with tier, section, perks, and price. Mark it as a trade — seeking a seat in another city — or a straight sale. No fee to list.

3

Deal

Make an offer — seat, cash, perks. Counter back and forth. When both sides confirm the agreed value, a payment hold is placed on both cards. No one gets charged unless there's a default.

4

Transfer

Send your ticket in your team's app, mark it sent. The other side confirms received. When both members confirm, holds are released and the deal closes. $1 flat fee on completed trades.
